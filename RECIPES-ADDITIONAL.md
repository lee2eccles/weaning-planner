# Additional Recipes — legume-free, 6–9 months

Additional recipes that widen the pool without widening the shopping list. See [PRD.md](PRD.md) §5.2. Every recipe here is:

- **Legume-free** under strict botanical exclusion — no pulses, beans, peas, green beans, edamame, peanuts or soy.
- **Age-appropriate for 6–9 months** — soft textures, finger-food or spoonable, no added salt, no added sugar, no honey, no whole nuts.
- **Ingredient-efficient** — deliberately built from the ingredient base the core recipes already establish (oats, eggs, milk, cheddar, cream cheese, Greek yoghurt, ricotta, parmesan, sweet potato, butternut, spinach, broccoli, cauliflower, courgette, carrot, sweetcorn, onion, garlic, stock, orzo, pasta, rice, cinnamon, cumin, lemon), so adding variety does not lengthen the shopping list proportionally.

Yields are given in **baby portions** as defined in PRD §5.6, where one baby portion is what one twin eats at one meal.

**Salt note:** where a recipe uses stock, it must be homemade or a low-sodium baby-suitable stock. Where a recipe uses two salty components (e.g. cheddar plus parmesan), it is flagged `saltAware` and the planner should avoid scheduling two such recipes on the same day.

## Breakfasts

### B1. Banana and Oat Pancakes
**Makes** 12 small pancakes (3 baby portions) · **V** · **Freezable** · Fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month

- 1 large ripe banana, mashed
- 2 eggs
- 60g porridge oats, blitzed to a rough flour
- 80ml whole milk
- ½ tsp baking powder
- pinch of ground cinnamon
- unsalted butter, for frying

1. Blitz the oats in a blender until they resemble a coarse flour.
2. Whisk the banana, eggs and milk together until smooth.
3. Fold in the oat flour, baking powder and cinnamon. Leave to stand for 5 minutes to thicken.
4. Melt a little butter in a non-stick pan over a medium heat. Cook tablespoon-sized pancakes for 1–2 minutes each side until set through and lightly golden.
5. Cool completely before storing. Freeze interleaved with baking parchment.

*Serve warm, cut into strips, with Greek yoghurt or mashed berries.*

### B2. Apple, Cinnamon and Yoghurt Overnight Oats
**Makes** 8 tbsp (2 baby portions) · **V** · **No-cook** · Not freezable · Fridge 2 days

- 40g porridge oats
- 120ml whole milk
- 1 small apple, grated (skin on or off)
- 60g Greek yoghurt
- pinch of ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp smooth almond butter — optional

1. Stir the oats, milk, apple, yoghurt and cinnamon together in a lidded tub.
2. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
3. Stir in the almond butter just before serving. Serve cold, or warm through gently.

*The oats soften completely overnight, giving a texture that needs no chewing. Ensure any nut butter is fully smooth.*

### B3. Butternut and Cheddar Egg Muffins
**Makes** 12 mini muffins (3 baby portions) · **V** · **GF** · **Freezable** · Fridge 3 days, freezer 1 month
**Special equipment:** 12-hole mini muffin tin

- 6 eggs
- 150g butternut squash, peeled and coarsely grated
- 50g cheddar, grated
- 1 spring onion, finely chopped
- small handful of parsley, finely chopped
- black pepper
- olive oil, for greasing

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C/gas mark 4) and grease the muffin tin well.
2. Whisk the eggs, then fold in the butternut, cheddar, spring onion, parsley and a little black pepper.
3. Divide between the holes and bake for 18–20 minutes until set and just golden.
4. Cool in the tin for 5 minutes before turning out.

*Excellent cold, and one of the best things in this list for eating one-handed on a school run or in a pram.*

### B4. Mango and Coconut Porridge
**Makes** 12 tbsp (3 baby portions) · **V** · **GF** (with gluten-free oats) · **Freezable** · Fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month

- 40g porridge oats
- 250ml whole milk
- 1 tbsp desiccated coconut
- pinch of ground cardamom or cinnamon
- 100g ripe mango, peeled and diced (frozen mango chunks work well)

1. Add the oats, milk, coconut and spice to a small saucepan over a medium heat. Cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring from time to time, until thick and creamy.
2. Mash or blend the mango and stir it through at the end.

*Cooks in the same pan and by the same method as the Butternut and Coconut Oats, so the two can be made back to back on prep day.*

### B5. Ricotta and Berry Toast Fingers
**Makes** 1 portion · **V** · **No-cook** (assembly) · Berry ricotta keeps 2 days in the fridge

- 1 slice of brown bread, crusts removed
- 40g ricotta
- 30g soft berries — raspberries or blueberries
- pinch of ground cinnamon

1. Mash the berries into the ricotta with the cinnamon.
2. Lightly toast the bread and spread thickly with the berry ricotta.
3. Cut into 6–7cm fingers and serve straight away.

*Blueberries must be squashed or halved — whole they are a choking hazard.*

### B6. Sweet Potato and Cinnamon Pancakes
**Makes** 14 small pancakes (3.5 baby portions) · **V** · **Freezable** · Fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month

- 150g cooked sweet potato, mashed
- 2 eggs
- 120ml whole milk
- 70g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- unsalted butter, for frying

1. Whisk the mashed sweet potato, eggs and milk together until as smooth as you can get it.
2. Fold in the flour, baking powder and cinnamon.
3. Cook tablespoon-sized pancakes in a buttered non-stick pan for 2–3 minutes each side.

*Chains directly with the roasted sweet potato from the Frittata Squares — roast one large sweet potato on prep day and it serves both recipes.*

## Lunches

### L1. Courgette, Lemon and Cheddar Orzo
**Makes** 4 baby portions · **V** · **Freezable** · Fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ small onion, finely chopped
- 1 medium courgette, coarsely grated
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 100g orzo pasta
- 300ml hot homemade or low-sodium vegetable stock
- 50g cheddar, grated
- zest of ½ lemon, plus a squeeze of juice

1. Soften the onion in the oil over a medium heat for 3 minutes.
2. Add the courgette and garlic and cook for a further 3 minutes until the courgette has collapsed.
3. Stir in the orzo, pour in the stock, and simmer for 10 minutes until the orzo is completely soft.
4. Beat in the cheddar, lemon zest and juice with a wooden spoon.

*Near-identical technique to the Tomato and Cheddar Orzo but a different flavour — cheap variety, since it adds only courgette and lemon to the list.*

### L2. Salmon, Sweet Potato and Dill Fishcakes
**Makes** 8 small cakes (4 baby portions) · **Freezable** · Fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month

- 200g sweet potato, peeled, boiled and mashed
- 150g salmon fillet, poached and flaked
- 1 spring onion, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- zest of ½ lemon
- 1 egg yolk
- 30g fine breadcrumbs, plus extra for coating
- olive oil, for frying

1. Combine the sweet potato, salmon, spring onion, dill, lemon zest, egg yolk and breadcrumbs.
2. Shape into 8 small patties and coat lightly in extra breadcrumbs. Chill for 15 minutes to firm up.
3. Fry in a little olive oil for 3 minutes each side until golden and hot all the way through.

*Freeze raw, on a tray, then bag — they cook from frozen in about 15 minutes at 190°C. Oily fish should be offered once or twice a week from 6 months.*

### L3. Cauliflower Cheese Pasta Bake
**Makes** 5 baby portions · **V** · **Freezable** · **saltAware** · Fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month

- 200g cauliflower, broken into small florets
- 100g small pasta shapes
- 20g unsalted butter
- 20g plain flour
- 300ml whole milk
- 60g cheddar, grated
- 20g parmesan or vegetarian hard cheese, grated
- pinch of nutmeg

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (fan 170°C/gas mark 5).
2. Boil the cauliflower for 8 minutes until very soft, adding the pasta to the same pan for the last 6 minutes. Drain.
3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Whisk in the milk a little at a time to a smooth sauce and simmer for 3 minutes.
4. Off the heat, stir in the cheddar and nutmeg. Fold through the pasta and cauliflower.
5. Tip into a dish, scatter with parmesan and bake for 15 minutes.

*Two cheeses makes this one of the saltier dishes in the library — the planner should not pair it with another saltAware recipe on the same day.*

### L4. Chicken, Leek and Sweetcorn Rice Pot
**Makes** 4 baby portions · **GF** · **Freezable** · Fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small leek, finely sliced and well washed
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 skinless boneless chicken thighs, finely diced
- 120g white rice
- 500ml hot homemade or low-sodium chicken stock
- 60g sweetcorn
- 1 tbsp cream cheese
- squeeze of lemon

1. Soften the leek in the oil over a medium heat for 5 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and chicken and cook until the chicken is white all over.
3. Stir in the rice, pour over the stock, cover and simmer for 18–20 minutes until the rice is very soft and the liquid absorbed.
4. Stir in the sweetcorn for the last 3 minutes. Off the heat, stir through the cream cheese and lemon.

*Uses the same chicken thighs as the Creamy Chicken and Broccoli Orzo — buy one pack, cook both.*

### L5. Carrot, Cumin and Cream Cheese Pasta
**Makes** 4 baby portions · **V** · **Freezable** (sauce) · Fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month

- 2 medium carrots, peeled and chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ onion, chopped
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- 200ml hot stock or water
- 80g cream cheese
- 100g small pasta shapes
- squeeze of lemon

1. Simmer or roast the carrots until completely soft.
2. Soften the onion in the oil, add the cumin and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Blend the carrots, onion, cumin and stock until perfectly smooth. Stir in the cream cheese and lemon.
4. Cook the pasta until very soft, chop it short, and fold through the sauce.

*The sauce freezes well on its own — cook fresh pasta on the day and the meal takes four minutes.*

### L6. Mushroom, Spinach and Parmesan Risotto
**Makes** 4 baby portions · **V** · **GF** · **Freezable** · Fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month

- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 150g chestnut mushrooms, very finely chopped
- 120g risotto rice
- 600ml hot homemade or low-sodium vegetable stock
- 2 large handfuls of spinach, finely chopped
- 30g parmesan or vegetarian hard cheese, grated
- squeeze of lemon

1. Soften the onion in the butter, then add the garlic and mushrooms and cook for 8 minutes until the mushrooms have released and reabsorbed their water.
2. Stir in the rice to coat, then add the stock a ladle at a time, waiting for each to be absorbed, until the rice is very soft — about 20 minutes.
3. Stir in the spinach for the last 2 minutes, then the parmesan and lemon.

*Deep and savoury without much effort, and it uses the mushrooms and spinach already on the list.*

### L7. Mini Beef and Butternut Meatballs with Chopped Spaghetti
**Makes** 16 small meatballs (4 baby portions) · **Freezable** · Fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month

- 250g beef mince (lamb also works)
- 100g butternut squash, finely grated
- 1 small onion, finely grated
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 30g fine breadcrumbs
- 1 egg yolk
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 200g passata or tinned chopped tomatoes
- spaghetti, to serve — around 30g per baby portion

1. Mix the mince, butternut, onion, garlic, breadcrumbs, egg yolk and oregano thoroughly. Roll into 16 small balls.
2. Brown in the oil for 5 minutes, turning.
3. Pour over the passata and simmer for 15 minutes until cooked through and the sauce has thickened.
4. Cook the spaghetti until very soft and snip into short lengths with scissors.

*The grated butternut keeps the meatballs soft enough to squash between gums — important at this age. Cut each meatball in half before serving; whole they are round and firm.*

### L8. Haddock, Leek and Sweetcorn Mash
**Makes** 4 baby portions · **GF** · **Freezable** · Fridge 2 days, freezer 1 month

- 300g potato, peeled and diced
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 small leek, finely sliced
- 200ml whole milk
- 150g **unsmoked** haddock or cod, cubed
- 60g sweetcorn
- small handful of chives, chopped

1. Boil the potato until completely soft, then drain.
2. Soften the leek in the butter for 5 minutes.
3. Pour in the milk, add the fish, and poach gently for 5 minutes until it flakes apart.
4. Add the sweetcorn and the potato and mash everything together to a soft, spoonable texture. Stir through the chives.

*Use unsmoked fish only — smoked haddock is far too salty for a baby. Check carefully for bones.*

---

### L9. Avocado, Cream Cheese and Cucumber Toast Fingers
**Makes** 1 portion · **V** · **No-cook** · Not freezable

- 1 slice of brown bread, crusts removed
- ¼ ripe avocado
- 1 tbsp cream cheese
- 20g cucumber, peeled, finely grated and squeezed dry
- squeeze of lemon

1. Mash the avocado with the cream cheese and lemon.
2. Fold in the grated cucumber.
3. Lightly toast the bread, spread thickly, and cut into fingers.

*The one genuinely zero-effort lunch in the library. Worth having in the pool so the planner can place it on days when nothing has been defrosted.*
